About My Solo Tools

I'm Nana building small tools for problems I keep running into.

Some come from my own work. Others come from Reddit, where someone is stuck on a file and the top reply is "just buy Acrobat." If I think a simple browser page could fix it, I build one.

Right now that means PDF tools. You can:

  • add a watermark before you send a document
  • pull out only the pages you need
  • split a long PDF into smaller files
  • add blank margin space for handwritten notes

Each tool does one job. That's the whole idea.

Your files stay on your computer. Everything runs in your browser, so nothing gets uploaded to a server. You can check this yourself: open a tool, turn off your Wi-Fi, and it still works.

I'll keep adding tools as I find problems worth fixing. If there's one you wish existed, let me know.

Thanks for stopping by.
